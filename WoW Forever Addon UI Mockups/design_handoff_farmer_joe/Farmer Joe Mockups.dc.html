1Farmer Joe: window directions, states and minimap button
Palette. The attached design system is empty, so everything uses the default WoW interface colours: gold #FFD100 headings, white body, grey #9D9D9D secondary, item-quality green and the standard orange/red warnings.
Fonts. Friz Quadrata and Arial Narrow are used when installed. Otherwise Marcellus and Archivo Narrow stand in for them.
Buildable. Frames, bars, pips and badges are flat fills and 1px edges, so each one maps to a backdrop or texture. The only round shapes are the coin icon and portrait ring, and both would be textures in game.
Sample player. Level 23 Warrior with Fishing and Mining. The farm data, prices and URLs are placeholders. The windows are clickable: you can select rows, toggle the filters and change bracket.
